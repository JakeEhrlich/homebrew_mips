Grit Block Diagram

One shared 16-bit data bus, one transfer per clock. Blue arrows are bus connections: toward the bus is a driver, away from it a latch that loads. Gold arrows are point-to-point and never shared. Each block lists the control lines it takes; every one of them comes from the control block.

A register read is two clocks: one to drive the index onto D[15:11] and load it into MAR, one for the SRAM to drive D into the latch. The write-back saves a clock because the second ALU clock leaves the bus idle and the index goes through then. Clocks per instruction: j 3, addiu and andi 8, addu, and, nor and beq 9, lw 10, sw 9.
