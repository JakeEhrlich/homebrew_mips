Grit Block Diagram

Two shared buses, one transfer per clock. Blue is the data bus: an arrow toward it is a driver, away from it a latch that loads. Gold is the address bus: the PC drives it during fetches, MAR otherwise; the three memories listen. Green paths are point-to-point. Each block lists the control lines it takes; every one of them comes from the control block.

The microprogram: what is active in each state

One driver per bus per state. A load line is a clock enable on the listed latch; the write strobes are the state gated with the clock's low half and the region decode.

A register read is two clocks: one to drive the index onto D[15:11] and load it into MAR, one for the SRAM to drive D into the latch. The write-back saves a clock because the second ALU clock leaves the data bus idle and the index goes through then. Clocks per instruction: j 3, addiu and andi 8, addu, and, nor and beq 9, sw 9, lw 10.
